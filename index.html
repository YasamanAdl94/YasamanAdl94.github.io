01 / RESEARCH

Research areas

My central question is whether a detector has learned evidence of manipulation or simply recognized the people and datasets it has already seen.

R.01

Identity-independent deepfake detection

Measuring and reducing identity leakage so models rely on manipulation artifacts instead of speaker-specific shortcuts.

Representation learningGeneralizationAudio forensics
R.02

Affective signals in synthetic speech

Studying emotion and affect as complementary evidence for detecting generated and manipulated speech.

Emotion2VecWav2Vec2
R.03

Cross-dataset robustness

Evaluating whether detection systems remain reliable across unseen attacks, recording conditions, and data distributions.

ASVspoofIn-the-Wild
R.04

Physiological signal analysis
